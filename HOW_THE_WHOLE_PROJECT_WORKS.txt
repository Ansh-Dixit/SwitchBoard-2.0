1. WHAT THIS PROJECT IS ABOUT (PROBLEM STATEMENT & MOTIVATION)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
In multilingual societies such as India, speakers naturally alternate between
Hindi and English within a single sentence — a phenomenon called **intra-sentential
code-switching (Hinglish)**. For example:

    "kal meeting ke baad main report submit kar dunga"
     [HI] [EN]    [HI] [HI] [HI] [EN]   [EN]   [HI] [HI]

Standard Automatic Speech Recognition (ASR) models such as pretrained Whisper
struggle on code-switched speech for three specific reasons:
  1) **Acoustic & Lexical Disruption at Switch Boundaries:** When a speaker
     transitions from a Hindi word to an English word (`HI -> EN`, e.g.,
     `"kal" -> "meeting"`) or from an English word back to Hindi (`EN -> HI`,
     e.g., `"meeting" -> "ke"`), the decoder's internal language prior frequently
     misrecognizes the boundary tokens.
  2) **Script Inconsistency (Devanagari vs. Latin):** In real Hindi–English
     corpora (such as MUCS 2021 Spoken Tutorials), English words are often
     written in Devanagari (`"मीटिंग"`, `"क्लिक"`, `"फाइल"`, `"ट्यूटोरियल"`,
     `"इम्प्रेस"`, `"स्लाइड"`) in some transcripts or ASR hypotheses and in Latin
     script (`"meeting"`, `"click"`, `"file"`, `"tutorial"`, `"impress"`, `"slide"`)
     in others. Standard WER tools treat `"इम्प्रेस"` and `"impress"` as a 100%
     word error even when the ASR recognized the exact spoken word.
  3) **Aggregate WER Hides Boundary Behavior:** Standard overall Word Error Rate
     (WER) pools all words together rather than isolating recognition accuracy at
     language-switch boundaries (`HI -> EN` and `EN -> HI`).

**SwitchBoard** solves these problems by combining:
  - A **Unified Dataset Pipeline** with reviewed word-level `HI`, `EN`, and
    `OTHER` tags and 16 kHz mono audio resampling (applied to your 3,136-utterance
    **MUCS 2021 Hindi–English corpus** across 30 speaker-disjoint splits).
  - A **Cross-Script Transcript Normalizer** that preserves raw transcripts while
    canonicalizing reviewed Devanagari/Roman spelling equivalents (`"मीटिंग" <-> "meeting"`,
    `"क्लिक" <-> "click"`, `"इम्प्रेस" <-> "impress"`) without losing reference
    word-to-language-tag alignment.
  - **Parameter-Efficient Fine-Tuning (PEFT LoRA)** on frozen `openai/whisper-small`
    decoder attention layers combined with an **Auxiliary Language Identification
    (LID) Head** on decoder hidden states and **3-Stage Curriculum Learning**.
  - A **Switch-Boundary Evaluation Engine** that separates **Boundary WER**
    (words immediately before and after a language switch) from **Non-Boundary WER**
    with deterministic edit attribution and Code-Mixing Index (CMI) analysis.


2. END-TO-END SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE & MODULE BREAKDOWN
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Below is how every folder and Python module in `SwitchBoard` fits together:

A. Dataset Preparation & Unified Manifest (`switchboard/data/`)
   - `switchboard/data/manifest.py`:
     * Defines `ManifestRecord`, containing all required canonical fields:
       `utterance_id`, `audio_path`, `original_transcript` (and `transcript`),
       `normalized_transcript`, `word_level_language_tags` (and `lang_tags` in
       `{"HI", "EN", "OTHER"}`), `speaker_id`, `accent`, `source`, `split`
       (`train`, `val`, `test`), `is_synthetic`, `duration_sec`, and `audio_sha256`.
     * `resample_to_16k_mono()`: Loads raw audio of any sample rate or channel
       count, averages channels to mono, resamples to 16,000 Hz using polyphase
       filtering (`scipy.signal.resample_poly`), saves 16-bit PCM WAV, and
       computes a deterministic SHA-256 audio fingerprint.
     * `validate_manifest_splits()`: Enforces four strict integrity rules:
       (1) `len(transcript.split()) == len(lang_tags)` for every utterance.
       (2) **Speaker Separation:** Zero `speaker_id` overlap across `train`,
           `val`, and `test`.
       (3) **Duplicate Recording Separation:** Zero shared audio paths or
           waveform SHA-256 hashes across splits.
       (4) **Synthetic & Demo Isolation:** Synthetic records (`is_synthetic=True`)
           are restricted to `split="train"`, and `forbid_demo_or_synthetic_in_research=True`
           blocks any demo or synthetic record from entering official research
           evaluations.

   - `switchboard/data/importers.py`:
     * `import_mucs2021_hi_en()`: Parses your extracted `Hindi-English_test.tar.gz`
       (`data/raw/mucs2021_hi_en/test/`), reading the 30 full lecture WAV files
       and `test/transcripts/{text, wav.scp, segments, utt2spk}`. It slices all
       **3,136 timestamped utterance segments** (`5.19 hours` of 16 kHz mono
       audio), tags every word as `HI`, `EN`, or `OTHER` using lexical tables
       (never relying on script alone), and partitions the 30 speakers into
       disjoint splits in `data/full_corpus/unified_manifest.jsonl`:
         - `train`: 2,021 utterances (20 speakers: 187 monolingual + 1,834 code-switched)
         - `val`:   598 utterances (5 speakers)
         - `test`:  517 utterances (5 held-out speakers: `mucs_834022`,
                    `mucs_847066`, `mucs_918821`, `mucs_921151`, `mucs_957491`)
     * Also implements tested importers for **IndicSUPERB Hindi Kathbath**
       (`import_kathbath_hi`), **Mozilla Common Voice Hindi & English**
       (`import_common_voice`), **Local Team Consented Recordings**
       (`import_local_team_recordings`, enforcing `consent_verified=true`),
       and the isolated **Demo Fixture** (`build_demo_fixture` -> `data/demo/`).

B. Transcript Normalization (`switchboard/normalization/normalizer.py`)
   - `TranscriptNormalizer`:
     * Preserves the verbatim `raw_transcript` and generates a normalized version
       for fair scoring.
     * Applies identical rules to both references and ASR hypotheses:
       (1) Unicode `NFC` normalization and zero-width joiner stripping.
       (2) Lowercase folding for Latin characters.
       (3) Punctuation stripping (ASCII punctuation + Devanagari purna viram `।`
           and double danda `॥`).
       (4) Lookup in `configs/normalization_equivalents.yaml` (190+ reviewed
           cross-script and Romanized spelling equivalents such as
           `"मीटिंग" -> "meeting"`, `"क्लिक" -> "click"`, `"इम्प्रेस" -> "impress"`,
           `"नोट्स" -> "notes"`, `"स्लाइड" -> "slide"`, `"ट्यूटोरियल" -> "tutorial"`,
           `"कल" -> "kal"`, `"दूँगा" / "doonga" -> "dunga"`).
     * `normalize_reference(transcript, lang_tags)` preserves 1-to-1 alignment
       between each normalized reference word and its original language tag
       (`HI`, `EN`, `OTHER`), while ensuring genuine lexical errors still count.

C. Baseline & Comparison Models (`switchboard/models/`)
   - `switchboard/models/registry.py` & `switchboard/models/runners.py`:
     1) **Primary Baseline (`WhisperBaselineRunner` — `openai/whisper-small`)**:
        * Pretrained 241.7M-parameter multilingual encoder-decoder (Apache-2.0).
        * Cached locally and used as both the primary baseline and the exact
          pretrained base checkpoint for PEFT LoRA fine-tuning.
     2) **Comparison Baseline 1 (`IndicWhisperRunner` — `vasista22/whisper-hindi-small`)**:
        * AI4Bharat Vistaar / Indic-tuned Whisper Small checkpoint for Hindi
          (MIT / CC-BY-4.0), cached locally and evaluated on the same held-out
          MUCS 2021 test utterances.
     3) **Comparison Baseline 2 (`Wav2Vec2CTCRunner` — `facebook/wav2vec2-xls-r-300m` & `Harveenchadha/vakyansh-wav2vec2-hindi-him-4200`)**:
        * Automatically inspects checkpoint architecture and reports that base
          `facebook/wav2vec2-xls-r-300m` is BLOCKED for direct ASR inference with
          `status = "requires_ctc_head_training"` (self-supervised encoder
          without a trained `lm_head` or `vocab.json`).
     4) **Fine-Tuned PEFT Runner (`WhisperPeftRunner`)**:
        * Loads frozen `openai/whisper-small` + trained PEFT LoRA adapter +
          optional Auxiliary Language Identification (LID) head. Includes an
          explicit guardrail rejecting any unit-test tiny fallback checkpoint.

D. Whisper Small Fine-Tuning: LoRA + Auxiliary LID Head + Curriculum Learning
   (`switchboard/training/whisper_lora_lid.py`)
   1) **Freezing Base Whisper & Targeting Decoder Attention with PEFT LoRA**:
      - All pretrained `openai/whisper-small` weights (`241,734,912` params) are
        frozen (`param.requires_grad = False`).
      - `find_decoder_attention_target_modules()` injects LoRA adapters
        (`1,179,648` trainable params for `r=8`, `< 0.5%` of total params)
        exclusively into the decoder self-attention and cross-attention linear
        projections (`q_proj`, `k_proj`, `v_proj`, `out_proj`).
   2) **Auxiliary Language Identification (LID) Head on Decoder Hidden States**:
      - `AuxiliaryLIDHead(d_model=768, num_labels=3)` (`297,987` trainable params)
        predicts `0: HI, 1: EN, 2: OTHER` from `decoder_hidden_states[-1]`.
      - `align_word_tags_to_subwords()` maps each reference word's language tag
        to its subword tokens while masking all prefix special tokens, EOS, and
        padding positions with `IGNORE_INDEX = -100`.
      - Joint objective: `L_total = L_ASR + (lid_loss_weight * L_LID)`.
   3) **Three-Stage Curriculum Learning & Checkpoint Reload Verification**:
      - Stage 1 (`monolingual`, CMI = 0) -> Stage 2 (`real_mixed`, CMI > 0) ->
        Stage 3 (`all_with_synthetic`, train split only).
      - Saves adapters and `lid_head.pt` to `checkpoints/whisper_small_lora_only`
        and `checkpoints/whisper_small_lora_plus_lid` and verifies exact reload
        equivalence (`asr_max_abs_diff = 0.0`, `lid_max_abs_diff = 0.0`,
        `reload_verified = true`).

E. Switch-Boundary Evaluation & Deterministic Edit Attribution
   (`switchboard/evaluation/boundary.py`)
   - Marks `pre_index` (`i`) and `post_index` (`i+1`) for every adjacent
     `HI -> EN` and `EN -> HI` switch; overlapping boundary words touched by two
     switches are counted **once** in the pooled boundary word set.
   - Aligns hypothesis to reference via Wagner-Fischer word-level edit distance
     and attributes insertions deterministically:
       * Beginning insertion ($i = 0$) -> reference position `0`
       * Internal insertion ($1 \le i < N$) -> preceding reference position `i - 1`
       * Ending insertion ($i = N$) -> last reference position `N - 1`
       * Empty reference ($N = 0$) -> utterance-level insertion (`op="UTT_INS"`, `attributed_ref_index=-1`)
   - Exports all 6 required research files to `outputs/full_benchmark_real/`:
     `per_utterance_metrics.csv`, `per_word_alignment.csv`,
     `aggregate_metrics.json`, `model_comparison.csv`,
     `error_analysis_breakdowns.csv`, and `evaluation_config.json`.


3. REAL EXPERIMENTAL MEASUREMENTS vs. ISOLATED DEMO / SMOKE OUTPUTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SwitchBoard enforces strict separation between three tiers of outputs:

  1) **Actual Model Benchmark Measurements (`outputs/full_benchmark_real/`)**:
     Evaluated via real PyTorch inference on 20 deterministic held-out MUCS 2021
     Hindi–English test utterances (`275` reference words: `103` switch-boundary
     words and `172` non-boundary words across all 5 unseen test speakers
     `mucs_834022`, `mucs_847066`, `mucs_918821`, `mucs_921151`, `mucs_957491`):

     - `openai/whisper-small (baseline)`:
       * Raw WER: `102.18% (281/275)` -> Norm WER: `97.82% (269/275)`
       * Norm CER: `81.59% (1259/1543)`
       * Boundary WER: `82.52% (85/103)` | Non-Boundary WER: `106.98% (184/172)`
       * RTF: `1.1837` (CPU)
     - `whisper-small + LoRA only` (6-step CPU smoke-trained adapter on real `openai/whisper-small`):
       * Raw WER: `107.64% (296/275)` -> Norm WER: `104.73% (288/275)`
       * Boundary WER: `91.26% (94/103)` | Non-Boundary WER: `112.79% (194/172)`
       * RTF: `1.5437` (CPU)
     - `whisper-small + LoRA + LID head` (6-step CPU smoke-trained adapter + LID head on real `openai/whisper-small`):
       * Raw WER: `106.18% (292/275)` -> Norm WER: `103.27% (284/275)`
       * Boundary WER: `93.20% (96/103)` | Non-Boundary WER: `109.30% (188/172)`
       * RTF: `1.8958` (CPU)
     - `indicwhisper (vasista22/whisper-hindi-small)`:
       * Raw WER: `77.82% (214/275)` -> Norm WER: `73.09% (201/275)`
       * Norm CER: `59.11% (912/1543)`
       * Boundary WER: `50.49% (52/103)` (`-32.04 pp` improvement over `openai/whisper-small` baseline!)
       * Non-Boundary WER: `86.63% (149/172)`
       * RTF: `3.6049` (CPU)

  2) **Smoke-Test Verification Outputs (`outputs/smoke_test/`)**:
     Real PyTorch inference on the 5-utterance held-out MUCS 2021 smoke subset
     (`59` reference words: `30` boundary words, `29` non-boundary words):
     - `openai/whisper-small (baseline)`: Norm WER `88.14%`, Boundary WER `43.33% (13/30)`
     - `whisper-small + LoRA only`: Norm WER `84.75%`, Boundary WER `40.00% (12/30)` (`-3.33 pp`)
     - `whisper-small + LoRA + LID head`: Norm WER `84.75%`, Boundary WER `40.00% (12/30)` (`-3.33 pp`)
     - `indicwhisper (vasista22/whisper-hindi-small)`: Norm WER `38.98%`, Boundary WER `30.00% (9/30)` (`-13.33 pp`)

  3) **Classroom Demo Simulation Fixture (`outputs/demo/`)**:
     Explicitly flagged with `is_simulated: true` and `SIMULATED DATA — NOT REAL MODEL RESULTS`.
     Isolated to `data/demo/` and `outputs/demo/` for demonstrating hand-calculated
     switch-boundary math in the classroom without contaminating research results.
